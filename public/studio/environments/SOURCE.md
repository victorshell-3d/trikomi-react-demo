# Kloppenheim 06

Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/kloppenheim_06
Downloaded asset: https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/HDRIs/hdr/2k/kloppenheim_06_2k.hdr
License: CC0, https://polyhaven.com/license

The 2k HDR is used locally for the ground-projected skybox and image-based reflections.
Projection camera height is 1.6 pedestal radii, projection radius is 80 pedestal radii.
A JPEG preview of this HDR is the loading background; the old room is not shown.
Ground projection is a flat approximation of the photographed terrain, not a reconstructed terrain mesh.
